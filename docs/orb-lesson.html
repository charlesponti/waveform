Orb Math — Interactive Lesson

Learn the math behind the voice orb, one step at a time. Drag the sliders and watch.

STEP 1

Points in space

A point in 3D is just three numbers: (x, y, z). x = left/right, y = up/down, z = toward/away from you. The center is (0,0,0).

The unit circle (sin & cos)

On a circle of radius 1, a point at angle θ has coordinates (cos θ, sin θ). Drag the angle to see the point move around the rim.

STEP 3

The unit sphere

A sphere of radius 1: every point satisfies x² + y² + z² = 1. We need a way to scatter dots evenly over its surface.

0.50
STEP 4

Fibonacci lattice

Golden angle = 137.5°. For dot i of n: y = 1 − 2(i+0.5)/n, r = √(1−y²), a = golden×i, then x=cos(a)·r, z=sin(a)·r. This spreads dots evenly with no clumps.

STEP 5

Age & pitch (latitude/longitude)

Latitude → age (0 at equator = newest sound, 1 at pole = oldest). Longitude → pitch (0 one side, 1 other). Color dots by age to see the mapping.

0.50
STEP 6

Push (gain)

Loudness v pushes each dot outward along its normal: new = old × (1 + gain·v·0.6). The 0.6 keeps it subtle. Drag gain to see the sphere bulge.

0.75 0.50
STEP 7

Spin (rotation)

Rotate around the vertical axis by spin·t. x1 = x·cos + z·sin, z1 = −x·sin + z·cos. Drag spin to control speed.

0.35
STEP 8

Tilt (the bulge angle)

Tilt around X by tip ≈ 0.45 rad. y2 = y·cos(tip) − z1·sin(tip). This is what you see vertically. Bigger tip = more ellipse/bulge.

0.45
STEP 9

Projection (3D → 2D)

Orthographic: drop the depth. screenX = cx + x1·R, screenY = cy − y2·R. The minus on Y is because screen Y grows downward.

0.45
STEP 10

Depth & layering

depth = (z2+1)/2 → 0 (back) to 1 (front). Dots bucketed into 4 layers, drawn back-to-front with increasing opacity. Front dots slightly larger. This sells the 3D illusion.

0.45
STEP 11

The full orb

All steps combined. Simulated loudness makes it breathe. Drag gain/spin/tip to feel the shape change.
